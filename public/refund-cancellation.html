Refund & Cancellation Policy

This policy applies to digital subscriptions for Tatv AI. Please review the plan, price, billing period and included usage before completing checkout.

1. Cancellation requests

You may request cancellation by emailing codefroge.academy@gmail.com from your account email. Include your plan name and Cashfree order or payment reference. We will confirm the cancellation status by email. Where a subscription is set to renew, a cancellation request should be submitted before the next renewal date to allow reasonable processing time.

2. Refund review

Because the Workbench provides digital access and usage-based features, a change of mind or unused allowance does not automatically qualify for a refund. However, verified duplicate charges, incorrect amounts, failed transactions that were nevertheless debited, or material service failures will be reviewed for an appropriate correction or refund. Any refund or remedy required by applicable law will be provided.

3. How to request a refund

Email codefroge.academy@gmail.com with your account email, transaction reference, date and a short explanation. Do not include card credentials, CVV, OTPs or UPI PINs.

4. Processing

After review and approval, refunds are initiated through the original payment method or payment provider process where available. The time taken for the amount to appear depends on the payment provider and issuing bank. We will communicate the decision and available next steps by email.

5. Contact

For cancellation or payment support, email codefroge.academy@gmail.com.
