Data Deletion Instructions

You can request deletion of your Dhwani AI account and personal information by following these steps.

How to submit a request

  1. Sign in to Dhwani AI, if you can access your account.
  2. Open your profile/settings and use the privacy links to review this page and the Privacy Policy.
  3. Email codefroge.academy@gmail.com with the subject “Dhwani AI data deletion request”. Include the email address associated with your account and clearly state what you want deleted.
Important: Do not send your password, one-time codes, OAuth tokens, or API keys in a deletion request.

What happens next

We may ask for reasonable information to verify that the request is yours. After verification, we will delete or de-identify personal information that we control, subject to applicable legal, security, and record-retention requirements. Information held by a third-party AI or social platform may need to be deleted directly through that provider as well.

Requests are handled as soon as reasonably practicable and within the timeframe required by applicable law. For questions about a request, email codefroge.academy@gmail.com.
