Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Dhwani AI handles information when you use its chat, AI, media, account, subscription and connected-service features.

Last updated: October 30, 2026

1. Who operates the service

Dhwani AI is an independently operated software workspace under the Dhwani AI name. This policy applies to information processed through the Workbench website and its features.

2. Information processed

Depending on the features you use, the Workbench may process:

  • Account information: email address, display name, account identifier and authentication/session information.
  • Profile information: optional age and profile image URL that you choose to provide.
  • Content you submit: chat prompts, conversation messages, files, images, media, URLs and other material you choose to provide for an AI or workspace feature.
  • Service and usage information: selected tools or providers, feature usage, subscription status, technical diagnostics, security events and information needed to enforce usage limits.
  • Payment records: subscription plan, amount, currency, Cashfree order and payment references, payment status and relevant timestamps.
  • Connection information: information needed to configure or operate a third-party connection when you choose to use one.

3. How information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts, provide chat and workspace features, process prompts and uploads, return generated results, manage subscriptions and usage allowances, provide support, prevent misuse, troubleshoot errors and maintain the service.

4. AI providers and other service providers

When you select or use an AI feature, the relevant prompt, file or task data may be sent to the provider configured for that feature. Depending on availability and your selection, the Workbench code supports Google Gemini, Cloudflare Workers AI, Pollinations and a configured FreeLLMAPI gateway for AI responses. Web-search requests may be sent to Tavily when that feature is configured. Video-generation requests may be sent to WaveSpeed when the feature is enabled.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices. Their retention and processing rules may differ. Review the relevant provider terms before submitting sensitive information. Do not upload information you do not have permission to share.

Supabase is used for account authentication and, when configured, database-backed account, subscription and usage records. The website is hosted by its configured hosting provider. These providers may process technical information required to deliver their services.

5. Payments and Cashfree

Subscription checkout is provided through Cashfree. The Workbench creates an order and receives payment confirmation details needed to verify the transaction and activate the selected subscription. The Workbench stores payment references and subscription records for account and billing administration.

Payment instrument details such as card credentials, UPI PINs and OTPs should be entered only in Cashfree's secure checkout. Dhwani AI does not ask you to send these credentials in chat or support messages. Cashfree processes payment instrument information under its own terms and privacy policy.

6. Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with the service providers described above when needed to provide a feature you request, secure or operate the Workbench, verify a payment, or comply with a lawful request. Information is not shared with a connected social account for publishing merely because you create a Workbench account; a connection or publishing action must be separately initiated and authorized where supported.

7. Security and retention

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect information, including server-side handling of provider secrets and authenticated account sessions. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Information is retained for as long as reasonably needed for the feature, account and subscription administration, security, dispute handling and applicable legal requirements. Retention may also depend on the third-party provider involved.

8. Your choices and data deletion

You can choose what prompts, files and optional profile information to submit. You may stop using optional AI or connected-service features. To request deletion of your account or personal information, follow the instructions on the Data Deletion page. We may need to verify your request. Some payment, security or transaction records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate record-keeping.

9. Children and age requirements

The Workbench is not intended for anyone below the minimum age required to use the service under applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please use the Data Deletion page to submit a request.

10. Changes and privacy questions

This policy may be updated when features, providers or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at this URL. For privacy questions, contact codefroge.academy@gmail.com. For account or personal-data deletion, follow the Data Deletion instructions. Never include passwords, one-time codes, API keys, access tokens, card details or UPI PINs in a request.
